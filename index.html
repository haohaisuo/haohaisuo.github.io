About

I am a PhD student at the School of Cyber Science and Technology, Shandong University. My research focuses on algebraic cryptanalysis of block ciphers and solving multivariate polynomial systems. I study methods based on Dixon resultants and Gröbner bases, as well as their efficient implementation, with applications to the security analysis of arithmetization-oriented cryptographic primitives.

中文简介

山东大学网络空间安全学院博士研究生。主要研究分组密码的代数分析与多项式系统求解，关注Dixon结式、Gröbner基等方法及其高效实现，并将其用于算术化友好密码原语的安全分析。
